Word Solitaire 1v1

Sort words into their categories. First to clear the board wins.

How to play
  • Put a category card into an empty home slot (top row) to open that category.
  • Send matching word cards onto it. When every word is home, the category clears and frees the slot.
  • In the columns, stack words on words of the same category. Any card can go into an empty column.
  • Tap the deck to draw. Tap a card to select it, then tap where it goes, or drag it. Double-tap sends it to the best spot.
  • 1v1: you both get the same deal. Whoever clears first wins. If time runs out, the higher score wins.
